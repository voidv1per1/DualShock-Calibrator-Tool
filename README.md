# 🎮 Controller Calibration Guide

Follow the steps below to properly calibrate your controller's analog sticks.

## 1. Open the Calibration Tool

Go to:

**https://dualshock-tools.github.io/**

Make sure your controller is connected to your PC.

## 2. Connect Your Controller

Once the website is open:

* Connect your controller via USB.
* Wait for the website to detect your controller.
* Make sure the correct controller is selected.

## 3. Calibrate Stick Center

1. Select **`Calibrate Stick Center`**.
2. Follow the instructions shown on screen.
3. Complete the calibration process.

> **Tip:** Keep the controller still when the tool asks you to do so.

## 4. Calibrate Stick Range

After completing the center calibration:

1. Select **`Calibrate Stick Range`**.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions step by step.
3. Once finished, check whether the joysticks are still moving on their own.

If the joysticks still appear to move, **feel free to redo the calibration test**.

## 5. Save Your Changes

Once both calibrations are complete:

1. Find the green **`Save Changes Permanently`** button.
2. Click it.
3. Wait for the changes to finish saving.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Make sure both the **Stick Center** and **Stick Range** calibrations are completed before saving.

6. Disconnect Your Controller

After everything has been saved:

1. Go to the top of the page.
2. Find the gray **`Disconnect`** button.
3. Click **`Disconnect`**.

Your controller calibration is now complete. ✅
